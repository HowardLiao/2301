04 · Platform

LITEON AI platform blueprint: complementing SAP, PLM and MES

Don't touch the core — augment it

Design principle: Keep the SAP core untouched; do not rebuild MES/PLM. The AI platform sits beside the three core systems, reading data, making recommendations and acting within authorized limits. Click each layer for details.

Division of labor with the three core systems

SystemTheir role: records and transactionsAI platform role: prediction and recommendation
SAP S/4HANAOrders, purchasing, inventory, financial postingsDemand forecasting, shortage alerts, anomalous transaction detection, month-end analysis
PLMBOM, ECN, drawing versionsDesign knowledge search, alternate-part recommendations, ECN impact analysis
MESWork-order execution, station records, traceabilityScheduling optimization recommendations, yield prediction, preventive equipment maintenance

Platform choice compares three routes — SAP-native, public cloud and open-source on-premises — on TCO and data sovereignty before approval, with no single vendor assumed.

Expected questions

For the CIO panel and the Chairman

Round 1 | CIO, technical advisor, CHRO

How do the AI platform and the S/4HANA go-live avoid interfering with each other?
Before 2027 Q1, only use cases that do not touch SAP; the platform reads via APIs with no core customization; supply-chain use cases follow once the go-live is stable.
How do you keep PoCs from dragging on?
Define the PO, go-live criteria and P and L line before starting; the quarterly committee retires use cases without value.
Should the platform be built or bought?
Compare three routes on TCO and data sovereignty; components can be bought, but data products and use-case knowledge must be owned in-house.
How would you lead AI technical leads?
Give clear architecture principles and decision rights; quarterly architecture reviews; every lead develops a successor.

Round 2 | Chairman

How much value can AI create for LITEON?
Start with three P and L lines — inventory, yield and cost of quality — and present an estimate confirmed by the CFO within 90 days.
What will LITEON’s AI look like in three years?
Every business group has its own AI products; new plants have predictive capability from day one; employees use AI as an everyday tool.
Why you?
Has done manufacturing SAP/MES integration, platform engineering and AI governance — and can write the architecture personally; speaks in business metrics.
